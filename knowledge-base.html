How it works运行方式

  1. BGE-M3 converts the question and reviewed chunks into vectors.BGE-M3 把问题和已核对知识块转换为向量。
  2. Cosine similarity retrieves the three closest evidence chunks.余弦相似度取回最相关的3条证据。
  3. Gemma 4 answers only from that evidence and cites it.Gemma 4 只根据这些证据生成回答并标注引用。

What it does not do它不会做什么

This is a retrieval prototype, not an approval system. It does not approve a WPS, select project parameters or replace the governing standard and Responsible Engineer.这是检索体验版，不是审批系统。它不能批准WPS、替项目选择参数，也不能替代适用标准和负责工程师。
